Problem & Concepts

What Basic Number Concepts brings to life

The Challenge

Fractions and place value are abstract symbolic relationships, and a printed figure cannot show a whole being split and recombined.

Concepts Covered

  • Fractions
  • Decimals
  • Odd & even numbers (parity)
  • Place value

The Storybook

Dinal and the Prism Temple follows a named protagonist through a continuous plot. Each page is both a narrative panel and an AR marker, so the story leads straight into the 3D scene.

Interactions

Direct, hands-on manipulation

Every scene uses the same tap-based model as the other components, with immediate bilingual visual and audio feedback.

Interaction 01

Odd or Even?

Numbered tiles appear beside the story character, and learners decide which numbers are odd or even by grouping 3D objects into pairs.

Interaction 02

Constructing Fractions

Learners split a 3D whole into equal parts and select pieces to build a given fraction.

Interaction 03

Place Value Blocks

Units, tens and hundreds are grouped and regrouped as 3D blocks to build a number.
